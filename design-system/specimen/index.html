Chapter one

How the gaps are measured

A label sits one cap height above the heading it belongs to. Paragraphs sit two cap heights apart, which is nearly double the space between lines inside a paragraph, so a new paragraph always reads as a break.

A new heading sits three cap heights below the text before it and two above the text after it, so it groups with what it introduces. Turn on text boxes to see the trimmed edges.

Big heroes

At the hero sizes, a heading's own line gap can be larger than two cap heights of body text. The floor rule keeps the gap below it at least as large as its own line gap.

克制的设计

每个项目都从同样的基础开始：网格、字号层级、间距阶梯，以及一小组颜色。规则一旦确定就不再反复讨论，时间留给真正属于这项工作的部分。

慢慢来，每一天都是新的开始

Chinese headings break only where <wbr> marks a phrase, so 每一天都是新的开始 never splits mid-phrase. Without <wbr>, a heading breaks only when it would overflow.

  1. Set lang on every Chinese or Japanese element: zh-Hans, zh-Hant or ja. The same character is drawn differently in each, and lang picks the right font.
  2. List the Latin font first, then the language's font. Latin letters stay in Inter and everything else falls through to the Chinese or Japanese font.
  3. Trim to the text box, not the cap height: text-box: trim-both text. Cap height and baseline are Latin measurements.
  4. Looser lines: body 1.75 (28px at 360, 32px at 1440), lead 1.6, headings 1.3.
  5. No negative letter-spacing and no italics. Emphasis uses weight.
  6. Japanese headings use word-break: auto-phrase so lines break between phrases where the browser supports it.

Images and media

Four ratios, all cropped from one source image. Widths follow the grid, heights follow the ratio.

  1. Four ratios: 16:9 by default, 4:3 for photos, 3:4 for portraits, 1:1 for thumbnails and avatars. 9:16 only for vertical video.
  2. Width follows the grid: full, wide, content, or a column span. Height comes from the ratio, never set by hand.
  3. Square corners. Avatars are round. Inside a rounded container, images follow the concentric rule.
  4. Reserve the space before the image loads, with aspect-ratio or width and height, so nothing jumps. Crop with object-fit: cover and set object-position on the subject.
  5. Captions use the caption role, 8px below the image, aligned to its left edge, at most 65 characters wide.
  6. Alt text says what the image shows. Decorative images get empty alt text.
  7. Video is 16:9 with a captions track, and never autoplays with sound.

Composition

How parts sit together. The bar at the top follows the same rules.

Row of controls

Show
  1. One size per row: md with a mouse, lg on touch.
  2. Label every group in a row, or none. Prefer controls that show their own meaning, like icons and color chips, and keep words only when no icon is clear.
  3. Labels sit to the left of controls, regular weight and muted. Values inside controls are medium weight. Labels above are for vertical forms.
  4. Icon-only controls are square, with a name for screen readers and a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus.
  5. Everything in a row shares one center line.
  6. 8px inside a group, 12px on touch. 24px between groups.
  7. Settings that apply immediately: a switch for one setting on or off, a segmented control to choose between 2 to 4 options, toggle buttons for independent tools. Checkboxes and radios are for forms.
  8. Selects look like inputs, with the system chevron.
  9. Text of different sizes side by side aligns on the cap top, the top edge of its trimmed box.
  10. Hairlines separate rows in tables and specimen lists only. Sections and reading text are separated by spacing alone.

Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor. Each rule is checked on this page.

  1. Contrast: text 4.5:1, controls and chart shapes 3:1, measured live in Color roles.
  2. Focus: a 2px ring on keyboard focus, and a skip link to the content as the first stop.
  3. Windows high contrast: anything that shows its state with a background (selected options, switches, checkboxes, tabs) switches to system colors so the state stays visible.
  4. Reflow: nothing scrolls sideways at 320px wide, except data tables inside their own scroll area.
  5. Text spacing: raising line height to 1.5 and letter-spacing to 0.12em clips nothing, because controls use minimum heights.
  6. Touch: 44px targets, and at least 12px between controls.
  7. Motion: everything will respect reduced motion. The rules arrive with the motion section.
  8. Keyboard: tabs, list boxes and menus move with arrow keys. The tabs and list on this page are visual only until the components are built.
